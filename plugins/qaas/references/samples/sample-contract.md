# Sample contract

No sample may be generated or changed until these facts are evidenced or user-confirmed:

- format, encoding, and schema/descriptor
- meaning of relevant fields and headers
- correlation-identifier rules
- mutable fields
- immutable/protected fields
- exact mutation rule per field
- create-new or modify-existing decision
- AI-editing or documented-generator permission
- expected system response and assertion
- nondeterministic fields and comparison strategy

Existing samples show project convention but do not prove field semantics. Missing or contradictory semantics remain blocking unknowns.

For binary or generated samples, bind the approved deterministic tool, source/input hashes, arguments, output path, and expected output hash or validation. Opaque transformations and improvised interpreter snippets are not allowed.

Record only non-secret content. Preserve encoding and line endings when relevant. Never overwrite an unapproved path or delete/move/rename the original.
